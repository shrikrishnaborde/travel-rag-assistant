# Wanderly Travels — Transport to Goa

Fares below are indicative, per person for flights/trains and per vehicle for cabs, in
Indian Rupees (INR), based on booking from Delhi/Mumbai/Bengaluru as the most common
origin cities. Actual fares vary with season and how far in advance the booking is made.

- **Flight** (Mumbai/Bengaluru → Goa, GOI): ₹3,500–₹6,500 per person, one way, ~1.5 hour flight.
- **Train** (Mumbai → Madgaon, Konkan Railway): ₹800–₹2,200 per person depending on class, ~10–12 hours.
- **Airport/station transfer cab** (up to 4 passengers): ₹800–₹1,200 one way.
- **Local self-drive scooter rental:** ₹400–₹600 per day.
- **Local taxi for full-day sightseeing** (up to 4 passengers, AC car): ₹2,500–₹3,500 per day.

## General Notes

- Children under 5 typically travel free or at a nominal charge on flights and trains;
  children aged 5–11 are usually charged at 50–75% of the adult fare. Exact child fares
  depend on the airline/railway and are confirmed at the time of booking.
- All private cab packages include a driver; driver accommodation and meals for
  multi-day trips are billed separately at approximately ₹500/day if not already
  included in the quoted package price.
