# Wanderly Travels — Goa Destination Guide

Goa is a coastal state on India's west coast, known for its beaches, Portuguese-era
architecture, nightlife, and seafood.

- **Best time to visit:** November to February (pleasant weather, 20–32°C). Avoid the
  monsoon season (June–September) for beach activities.
- **North vs. South:** North Goa (Baga, Calangute, Anjuna) is popular for nightlife and
  water sports. South Goa (Palolem, Colva, Benaulim) is quieter and better for families.
- **Nearest airport:** Goa International Airport (GOI), Dabolim.
- **Nearest railway station:** Madgaon Junction.
- **Getting around:** Scooter/bike rentals and taxis; pre-paid taxi counters are
  available at the airport.
- **Documents:** A valid government photo ID is required for hotel check-in and for
  renting a two-wheeler.
