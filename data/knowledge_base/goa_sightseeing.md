# Wanderly Travels — Goa Sightseeing & Activity Packages

Each entry lists the activity, approximate duration, indicative price per person (INR),
and the interest category it maps to (Sightseeing, Adventure, Beaches, Shopping,
Religious).

- **Anjuna Flea Market & Local Shopping Tour** — half-day, ₹400/person (transport only; shopping spend extra) — *Shopping*
- **Dudhsagar Waterfall Jeep Safari** — full-day, ₹1,800/person — *Adventure, Sightseeing*
- **Sunset Cruise on the Mandovi River** with live music — 2 hours, ₹900/person — *Sightseeing, Beaches*

## Package Bundling

Sightseeing activities are typically bundled 1–2 per day within a package itinerary so
travellers are not overscheduled; full-day activities usually replace all other
sightseeing for that day.
