# Wanderly Travels — Booking, Cancellation, and Refund Policies

## Booking Confirmation

A booking is confirmed only after the 25% advance payment is received and a
confirmation voucher is issued by email. Hotel and transport bookings are subject to
availability at the time of advance payment, not at the time of initial quotation.

## Cancellation Policy

- Cancelled **30+ days** before travel: full refund of advance payment minus a ₹1,000 processing fee per booking.
- Cancelled **15–29 days** before travel: 50% of the advance payment is refunded.
- Cancelled **7–14 days** before travel: 25% of the advance payment is refunded.
- Cancelled **less than 7 days** before travel, or no-show: no refund.
- **Houseboat bookings (Kerala)** follow a stricter policy due to operator terms:
  cancellations within 10 days of the cruise date are non-refundable regardless of the
  general policy above.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days to the original payment method.
Refunds for credit card payments may take an additional 5–7 business days to reflect on
the customer's statement due to bank processing times.

## Date Change / Rescheduling

Date changes are permitted free of charge if requested at least 15 days before the
original travel date, subject to hotel and transport availability at the new dates
(fare or rate differences, if any, are payable by the customer). Date changes
requested within 15 days of travel are treated as a cancellation and re-booking, and
the cancellation policy above applies.

## Travel Documents and ID

All travellers must carry a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, passport, voter
ID, or driving licence) for hotel check-in and domestic flights. For travellers under
18, a birth certificate or school ID is accepted in addition to a parent's ID.

## Force Majeure

In case of cancellations due to circumstances beyond Wanderly Travels' control (natural
disasters, government travel restrictions, airline/train strikes), the standard
cancellation policy is waived and customers are offered either a full credit note valid
for 12 months or a refund minus only the non-recoverable third-party costs already paid
to hotels/transport providers.

## Travel Insurance

Travel insurance is not included in any package by default and is strongly
recommended, especially for hill station and adventure activity packages. Wanderly
Travels can arrange third-party travel insurance on request for an additional cost,
quoted separately from the package price.
