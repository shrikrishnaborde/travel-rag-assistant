# Wanderly Travels — Kerala Destination Guide (Munnar & Alleppey)

Kerala's backwaters and hill stations are among South India's most popular leisure
circuits. Munnar (hill station, tea plantations) and Alleppey/Alappuzha (backwaters,
houseboats) are commonly combined into a single itinerary.

- **Best time to visit:** September to March. The region receives heavy monsoon rain
  from June to August.
- **Nearest airport for both regions:** Cochin International Airport (COK).
- **Houseboat cruises:** Typically an overnight stay (check-in around noon, check-out
  around 9am the next day), with all meals included.
- **Travel times from Cochin:** Munnar is roughly a 4-hour drive; Alleppey is roughly a
  1.5-hour drive.
