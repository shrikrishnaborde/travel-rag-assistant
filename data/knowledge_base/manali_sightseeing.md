# Wanderly Travels — Manali Sightseeing & Activity Packages

Each entry lists the activity, approximate duration, indicative price per person (INR),
and the interest category it maps to (Sightseeing, Adventure, Beaches, Shopping,
Religious).

- **Solang Valley Adventure Package** (paragliding, zorbing, ropeway) — half-day, ₹2,200/person — *Adventure*
- **Hadimba Temple & Old Manali Walking Tour** — half-day, ₹300/person — *Sightseeing, Religious*
- **Rohtang Pass Excursion** (permit required, seasonal, snow activities) — full-day, ₹2,500/person including permit — *Adventure, Sightseeing*
- **Mall Road Shopping & Local Cafe Walk** — half-day, self-guided, no fixed charge — *Shopping*
- **Kasol & Manikaran Day Trip** (river valley, Gurudwara) — full-day, ₹1,600/person — *Sightseeing, Religious*

## Package Bundling

Sightseeing activities are typically bundled 1–2 per day within a package itinerary so
travellers are not overscheduled; full-day activities usually replace all other
sightseeing for that day.
