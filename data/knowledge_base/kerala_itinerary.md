*Suitable for: couples or families of up to 4, budget around ₹60,000–₹70,000, interests
in sightseeing and relaxation.*

- **Hotel:** Misty Hills Resort, Munnar (Standard), 1 room × 2 nights = ₹6,800; Premium Houseboat, Alleppey (Deluxe), 1 night = ₹9,000
- **Transport:** Round-trip flight Bengaluru–Cochin–Bengaluru, 2 passengers ≈ ₹12,000; private cab package Cochin–Munnar–Alleppey–Cochin ≈ ₹10,500
- **Sightseeing:** Munnar Tea Plantation & Tea Museum Tour (₹500 × 2 = ₹1,000); Eravikulam National Park (₹450 × 2 = ₹900); Alleppey Village & Canal Canoe Tour (₹700 × 2 = ₹1,400)
- **Meals & miscellaneous:** ≈ ₹5,500 (houseboat meals are included in the houseboat rate)
- **Estimated Total: ~₹47,100 for 2 travellers** (scales up with an extra room for a family of 4)

**Day-wise plan:**
1. Arrive in Cochin, drive to Munnar, check-in, evening at leisure.
2. Munnar Tea Plantation Tour and Eravikulam National Park.
3. Drive to Alleppey, board houseboat by noon, overnight cruise.
4. Disembark houseboat by 9am, Alleppey Village & Canal Canoe Tour, drive to Cochin airport area.
5. Departure.
