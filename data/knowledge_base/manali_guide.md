# Wanderly Travels — Manali Destination Guide

Manali is a hill town in Himachal Pradesh at an altitude of about 2,050 meters,
popular for snow activities, trekking, and scenic valleys.

- **Best time to visit:** March to June for pleasant weather; October to February for
  snow. Roads via Kullu can be affected by landslides during the monsoon (July–August)
  — travel isn't recommended on heavy monsoon days.
- **Nearest airport:** Bhuntar (Kullu-Manali) Airport, about 50 km away with limited
  flight frequency. Flying into Chandigarh and taking a 7–8 hour road journey is the
  more reliable option.
- **Nearest railway station:** Joginder Nagar (narrow gauge, limited connectivity) —
  most travellers reach via Chandigarh or Delhi by road.
- **Pack for:** Warm clothing year-round — nights are cold even in summer.
