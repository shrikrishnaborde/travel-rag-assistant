*Suitable for: families of 4, budget around ₹45,000–₹50,000 total, interests in
beaches and light sightseeing.*

- **Hotel:** Sea Breeze Inn, Colva (Budget) — 2 rooms × 3 nights = ₹10,800
- **Transport:** Round-trip train (Mumbai–Madgaon–Mumbai), 4 passengers ≈ ₹6,400; airport/station transfers ≈ ₹1,600
- **Sightseeing:** North Goa Beaches & Forts Tour (₹700 × 4 = ₹2,800); South Goa Heritage Tour (₹600 × 4 = ₹2,400); Water Sports Combo for 2 adults (₹1,500 × 2 = ₹3,000)
- **Meals:** Breakfast included in hotel rate; lunch/dinner estimated ₹800/day for the family ≈ ₹2,400 for 3 days
- **Local taxi** (2 days full-day sightseeing): ₹2,500 × 2 = ₹5,000
- **Miscellaneous & service charges:** ₹3,000
- **Estimated Total: ~₹47,400 for a family of 4**

**Day-wise plan:**
1. Arrival, check-in, relax at Colva Beach, evening at leisure.
2. North Goa Beaches & Forts Tour, water sports for adults, dinner at a beach shack.
3. South Goa Heritage Tour, free time for shopping, farewell dinner.
4. Check-out and departure transfer.
