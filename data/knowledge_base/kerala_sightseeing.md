# Wanderly Travels — Kerala Sightseeing & Activity Packages (Munnar & Alleppey)

Each entry lists the activity, approximate duration, indicative price per person (INR),
and the interest category it maps to (Sightseeing, Adventure, Beaches, Shopping,
Religious).

- **Munnar Tea Plantation & Tea Museum Tour** — half-day, ₹500/person — *Sightseeing*
- **Eravikulam National Park** (Nilgiri Tahr spotting) — half-day, ₹450/person including park entry — *Sightseeing, Adventure*
- **Alleppey Backwater Houseboat Cruise** — overnight, included in the houseboat hotel package (see the Premium Houseboat listing) — *Sightseeing, Beaches*
- **Alleppey Village & Canal Canoe Tour** — half-day, ₹700/person — *Adventure, Sightseeing*
- **Munnar Spice Market Shopping Walk** — half-day, self-guided, no fixed charge — *Shopping*
- **Chottanikkara Temple Visit** (near Cochin) — half-day, ₹350/person — *Religious*

## Package Bundling

Sightseeing activities are typically bundled 1–2 per day within a package itinerary so
travellers are not overscheduled; full-day activities usually replace all other
sightseeing for that day.
