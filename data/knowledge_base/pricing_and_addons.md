# Wanderly Travels — Pricing Guidelines and Add-ons

## Package Pricing Structure

A travel package price is generally built from five components: (1) hotel
accommodation, (2) transport to/from the destination, (3) local transport and
transfers, (4) sightseeing and activities, and (5) a service charge covering agent
commission and support.

## Service Charge

Wanderly Travels applies a service charge of **8%** of the subtotal (hotel + transport
+ sightseeing) for Standard and Budget category packages, and **10%** for Deluxe and
Luxury category packages. This is already reflected in the "Estimated Total" figures
in the Sample Itineraries document.

## Seasonal Pricing

- **Peak season** (mid-December to mid-January, and major holiday weeks): hotel rates
  increase by 20–30% over each hotel's base rate listed in its own hotel document.
- **Shoulder season** (February–March, October–November): base rates apply.
- **Off-season** (April–June for hill stations, June–September for Goa/Kerala monsoon):
  hotel rates may be discounted 10–20%, but some activities (e.g. water sports, Rohtang
  Pass access) may be unavailable.

## Group and Child Discounts

- Groups of 8 or more travellers booking the same package receive a **5% discount** on
  the sightseeing and local transport components.
- Children below 5 years are not charged for sightseeing activities or local
  transport; children aged 5–11 are charged at **50%** of the adult rate for
  sightseeing activities.

## Payment Terms

- A **25% advance payment** is required to confirm a booking.
- The remaining 75% balance is due no later than 15 days before the travel start date.
  For bookings made within 15 days of travel, full payment is required at the time of
  booking.
- Payments can be made via bank transfer, UPI, or credit/debit card. A 2% processing
  fee applies to credit card payments.

## Budget Fitting Guidance for Agents

When a customer's stated budget does not comfortably fit a destination and duration,
agents should try, in order:
1. Downgrading hotel category by one tier.
2. Switching from flight to train/bus transport.
3. Reducing the number of paid sightseeing activities.
4. Shortening the trip by one day.

Only after exhausting these options should the request be described as infeasible to
the customer.
